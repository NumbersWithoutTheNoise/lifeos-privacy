Life OS — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Life OS does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no server belonging to us. Nothing you write in the app is sent anywhere.

Where your information lives

Everything you enter — goals, projects, tasks, notes, journal entries, habits, recipes, reading list, memories, money entries, images and drawings — is stored on the device you entered it on, in the app's own storage. We cannot see it.

Information the app reads from your device

  • Calendars. With your permission, Life OS reads the calendars already set up on your device so your events can appear beside your goals, and can add events you create. Calendar data is read and written on the device only. It is never copied, uploaded, or sent to us.
  • Photos. When you attach a picture, you choose it yourself through the system photo picker. Only the picture you pick is copied into the app. Life OS has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Location. If you use the places feature, Life OS asks for your location to show which saved places are near you. It is used on the device at that moment and is never stored or transmitted.

Optional sync you control

Life OS can write a copy of your data into a folder you choose, so your own devices can share it through a service you already use, such as iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. This is off unless you turn it on and pick the folder. The data goes only to that folder, under whatever privacy terms your chosen service provides. It does not reach us.

Backups you make yourself

You can export your data to a file. That file goes wherever you save it, and is yours to keep or delete.

Children

Life OS is a personal organiser and is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no data, none is collected from anyone.
